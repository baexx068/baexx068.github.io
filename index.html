02 / Ongoing projects

What we are working on

Wearables · Frailty

Wearable signals for exercise capacity in frail older adults

Do submaximal stair-climb heart rate and accelerometry add to clinical tests when estimating maximal ergometer load after open-heart surgery? Built as a reproducibility and multiverse analysis.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
PhysioNet frailty dataset → Polar H10 ECG + ACC → HR & activity features → Multiverse models → Max load estimate
Device validation · Sarcopenia

Machine-learning calibration of a gait speedometer

Linear versus machine-learning calibration of an automated gait speed device in older adults, tested for agreement, AWGS 1.0 m/s classification and auto-trigger failure detection.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
Gait speedometer → Standing / flying start → Linear vs ML calibration → Agreement analysis → AWGS classification
Population health

Muscle-strengthening activity, distress and obesity

How muscle-strengthening activity, sitting time and sleep relate to psychological distress (PHQ-4) in US adults, and whether obesity modifies those links. A second study examines physical activity guidelines in cancer survivors.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
HINTS 7 national survey → Survey-weighted models → Effect modification → Obesity × PHQ-4
Cohort · Aging

AI trajectories of frailty and sarcopenia

Longitudinal modeling across six waves of the Korean Frailty and Aging Cohort, using repeated gait speed and DXA body composition to predict frailty and sarcopenia trajectories.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
KFACS 6 waves → Gait speed + DXA → Sequence models → Risk trajectories
Computer vision · Body composition

Smartphone photos to body fat percentage

A measurement-protocol study estimating InBody body fat percentage from standardized torso photographs, with BMI as the comparison baseline and tape and skinfold measures added.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
Smartphone photos → Torso crop → CNN regression → Body fat %
Rehabilitation technology

Sensor-based smart wheelchair training

A randomized pilot and feasibility trial of a sensor-instrumented wheelchair training platform, alongside a usability evaluation of a wheelchair driving simulator.

DesignDataAnalysisManuscript
Instrumented wheelchair → Training simulator → Feasibility & usability → Rehab outcomes

Results are shared here after peer-reviewed publication.
